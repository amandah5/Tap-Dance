^Repeat, but turn out & DOUBLE TIME IT

^Repeat, but turn out & DOUBLE TIME IT

Traveling R: both feet together. Extra slow crawls x4, moderate crawls x8

Traveling L: separate the feet. 8 moderate crawls, 16 fast crawls
