CLASS 3: LYRICAL (12+): 6:00-7:00

6:02-6:15: follow-along warmup: Dear August, I’m with You, Simply The Best, Girl, Nobody Knows, Carry You, Up Up & Away, breathe again, Dream, Quietly Yours, Easy On Me
* Start with breath in every body part
* Gentle stretch


6:15-6:21: weight shift balance exercise: Hold My Girl
* DON’T START UNTIL 0:34 OR LATER (THERE WILL BE SOME EXTRA 4-COUNTS)
* Jump out ball change to wide 2nd turned out, scoop together to sous-sus, lift R to passe, fall fwd onto it & coupe L behind 
* 2 enveloppes traveling back, step L, tendu R to prep 
* Pique fwd onto R & arabesque L HOLD HOLD HOLD return. Same to the side (lateral), same to the back (layout almost?). KEEP IT MOVING BUT GROUNDED
* Instead of doing the side again: fall ball change fwd, side, back, step together
* Repeat L


*** 6:21-6:26: ATF #1: Simple Song 
* It moves! Make it look efffortless
* Drag fwd peeling arms up, ball change to drop down, shoot up
* Extend R foot fwd in plie, swing around to swoop into soutenu
* Pique turn with diag arms traveling BACK where you came from, 3 steps out of it
* Leap onto R & swoop L around rond, cross out ball change step out other way ball change (5&6&7&8&)


*** 6:26-6:31: ATF #2: Truly Madly Deeply
* Beautiful pique arabesque, step step
* Chug R passe L, chug L passe R
* Pique back on L & parallel passe R, scoop upper body up & over, ball change, FAST CHAINES
* CONTROL coming out of the chaines to start again


6:31-6:35: ATF #3: What Would I Do Without You
* 2 walks traveling back, head knocks back for an up up down to continue back
* Shift spot & keep weight on R foot to go into a back attitude float turn
* Step down L, melt into a lunge L as you step R foot out
* Stay in lunge but reach up toward that corner, then sweep arms to floor for a lil handstand scoot
* Continue momentum in that direction, turn with L sickling behind 
* Lil passe hoppy thing to bring L fwd again
* Keep turning over R shoulder to start again


*** 6:35-6:38: ATF #4: Days Like This
* BIG chasse side with arms coming up, press to releve turned out 2nd with arms sweeping fwd, grab to plie, single coupe pirouettte SUSTAINED right from that 2nd
* Step R, lil tappy L behind, chasse L, high floaty inside fan kick R, step fwd, then step back out of it to start again


6:38-6:40: water break


6:40-7:00: combo: Heart’s Content
* DISCUSS
   * I’m all for an angsty contemp combo BUT this is lyrical. We’re telling a story based on story that the music tells, not just the SOUNDS in the music
   * The story of this song: the highs and lows of love. You and me
* Hold 2 moderate 8s
* We start right with the lyrics
* Step fwd to releve on “maybe”, hugging yourself 
* Touch R out & drop down to come up on “you thought”
* 5678: scoop R arm as you step R side & L behind, then touch R out again as L hand reaches up & grabs down
* 12&3: press releve L & a low back attitude as hands hit 3rd. Cross front side back to land in croise lunge
* 5678: reach R arm upstage corner (opening hips), then return to croise & plie contract in turned out 4th with clasped hands
* Bring feet to 5th, rotate to parallel & lift upper body, lil sways to reach on low diagonal L then R then rotate to L tendu devant efface (keeping 2 low jazz hands)
* 2 lil pique extends to the corner, pique L to fan R high, low chaine out of it
* Big scoop to jump into a curtsy position
* On “-ver lie” bring hands to mouth R then L, then shoot them both forward
* Tuck L foot, gently lower hands to floor, sweep R leg & arm around to end on knees facing stage R on count 1
* L hand to platter on “our”, R arm swoops back to meet it on “tongues” 
* Grab fists, rotate arms lil circly swoop to L, sit onto L hip & roll
* Instead of a normal stand up: lil handstand shooting R attitude to the ceiling on “love”
* On “fall out” just 2 sharp shoulder rolls back, L  then R, standing in a wide 2nd facing front
* Prep L, double pirouette in passe with R arm snaking up. Step R & drag toward R, looking back at L, then fall into runs leading up to the chorus
* 0:43 CHORUS: on “here’s” lift to a releve with arms raising up to a diagonal facing upstage R corner, ball change step LRL “to you and”. On “me” lil step together step leading with R shoulder, quick ball change step L with R coming across on 7 8 arms diag to jackknife.
* Step out L with a sway slicing R arm fwd, then out R slicing L arm fwd (this is AFTER “between”), pas de bourree 
* Pique up to parallel passe with arms in straight line (it just said “draw a line”), step down L, step R to meet it & slice around over R shoulder with feet together
* Pique L big sway lifting R foot slightly degage side, 2 steps, same thing R but break partway through, 2 more steps
* Neutral feet facing fwd: head, grab knee, push fwd, rotate to diag, hands to heart & head switch, hands to heart & head switch again, 2 chugs around with L foot, step R in & release opening the chest
